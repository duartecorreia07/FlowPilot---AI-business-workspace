# GLOSSARY

This section defines key terms used throughout the project.

## Service Order

A record representing a customer's request for service, containing multiple items with individual processing statuses.

## Item

A physical object being processed as part of a service order, with attributes like quantity, condition, and processing steps.

## Shipment

A record tracking the movement of items between locations, including tracking numbers, carrier information, and delivery dates.

## Location

A facility (workshop, warehouse, storefront) where inventory is stored or services are performed.

## Inventory

Location-aware stock management system tracking available, reserved, and consumed quantities.
